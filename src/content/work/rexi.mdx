## The problem

<p class="cause redact" data-beat="cause">LLMs are good at language. They are bad places to store business truth.</p>

## The boundary

The model reads the caller and proposes an intent. The server owns everything that can be wrong in a
way that costs money: menu validation, price calculation, cart mutation, quote generation and confirmation.
